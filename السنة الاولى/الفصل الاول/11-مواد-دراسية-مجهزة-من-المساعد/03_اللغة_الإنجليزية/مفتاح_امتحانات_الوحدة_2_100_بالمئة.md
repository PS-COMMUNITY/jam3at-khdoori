### س5 — أدوات الربط (until, because, so, while, when):
| الجملة | الإجابة |
|--------|---------|
| 1. They didn't start making the film ___ two years later. | **until** |
| 2. Feifer didn't have his personal copy of the book ___ a friend lost it. | **because** |
| 3. Hopkins wanted to read the book ___ he went to London to buy a copy. | **so** |
| 4. ___ he was waiting for his train home, he saw a book on a seat near him. | **While** |
